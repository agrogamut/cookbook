How to read a recipe page

Each page states its own selection reasons -- the active nutrition target, the region match and the food practice -- taken from the engine's step-by-step record rather than summarised here. The nutrition figures are totals for the ingredients listed, rebuilt from the IFCT 2017 food composition tables, and each one says what proportion of the dish stands on a measured value rather than on a food-group planning figure.

Safety

Age suitability and allergy exclusion are absolute filters. A recipe excluded for an allergen is not in this book and cannot be put back into it. Nothing on these pages has completed culinary, nutrition or clinical review.
